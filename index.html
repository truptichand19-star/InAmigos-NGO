About InAmigos Foundation

Founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh and works across India We work across India in education, women empowerment, environment protection, animal welfare, food distribution, and skill development.
